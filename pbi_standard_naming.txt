Names in English

all lower case, and joined with _ for column names

CustomerName --> customer_name
Order Date --> order_date
shipcountry --> ship_country

Table prefixes for power bi tables
fact_ for fact tables
dim_ dimension tables

Column suffixes
_id a unique identifier from the dataset itself
_key a unique identifying surrogate key for Power Bi

Readable names minimize abbreviations
ord_st_cd --> order_status
cust-nm --> customer_name
